— Why Shravan Matters

The month Lord Shiva
saved the universe.

During the Samudra Manthan — the great churning of the cosmic ocean — a deadly poison, Halahal, emerged with the power to destroy all of creation. To protect the universe, Lord Shiva consumed the poison and held it in his throat, which turned blue. This is why he is known as Neelkanth.

Every Monday of Shravan — Shravan Somvar — devotees offer water and milk to the Shivling to cool and honour this sacrifice. It is considered the most powerful time of the year to seek Mahadev's grace, release doshas, and begin spiritual practice with sincerity.

Ritual I

Shravan Somvar Vrat Puja

Weekly Monday fasting and Vedic worship dedicated to Lord Shiva — performed across all four sacred Mondays of the month.

Ritual II

Rudrabhishek Puja

Ritualistic bathing of the Shivling with Gangajal, milk, ghee and Panchamrit — cleansing misgivings and promoting spiritual growth.

Ritual III

Parthiv Shivling Puja

Worship of a sacred earthen Shivling — Dugdh Abhishek for prosperity, and Madhu Abhishek across three Mondays for relief from chronic ailments.
