About Pandit Ji

Pandit Umesh
Mishra.

A 26-year-old dedicated Vedic priest with his roots in the time-honoured North Indian tradition. Pandit Umesh Mishra completed his education in the Vedas, Sanskrit Grammar and Jyotish Shastra at the Maharishi Mahesh Yogi Vedic University, Jabalpur (M.P.), spending his formative years in deep study of the scriptures.

He has served devotees across New Delhi (2004–2009) and Bhopal (2009–2015) before making Pune his home. Whether it is Vedic astrology, Vastu Shastra or a full ritual ceremony, each ritual is performed personally — never outsourced — with correct mantra, muhurat and vidhi.

His mission is simple — to help every family find peace, prosperity and spiritual well-being through the authentic practice of the Vedas.
