Shrimad Bhagwat Saptaha Katha

Seven sacred days — the most powerful recitation in the Vedic tradition.

Shrimad Bhagwat Saptaha is the complete seven-day recitation of the 18,000 sacred verses of the Bhagwat Puran. The shastras declare that in Kaliyug, hearing the Bhagwat with sincerity destroys all sins, releases ancestral debts, and opens the path to moksha. Each day opens with Kalash Sthapana and puja; the seventh day closes with a Purnahuti havan — conducted entirely by Pandit Ji with full Vedic procedure.
